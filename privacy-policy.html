Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this personal Pinterest MCP / AI connector application handles information when it is used with a Pinterest account.

1. Purpose of the application

This application is a personal-use integration intended to connect an authenticated Pinterest account with an AI assistant or MCP-compatible client. It may be used to create, schedule, publish, and manage Pins and to work with the authenticated user's own Pinterest boards and related content.

2. Information accessed

When authorized by the user, the application may access Pinterest account information and Pinterest content permitted by the granted API scopes, including the user's own Pins and boards, and information required to create, schedule, publish, or manage that content.

The application is not intended to access or manage content belonging to other Pinterest users except where Pinterest's API and the user's granted permissions explicitly allow it.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested Pinterest integration features, such as creating Pins, publishing or scheduling Pins, reading the user's own boards and Pins, and managing content through the authorized Pinterest API.

4. Sharing and sale of information

Personal information is not sold or rented. The application does not intentionally share Pinterest user data with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure required to operate the integration, subject to applicable terms and security practices.

5. Authentication and access tokens

Authentication is performed through Pinterest's authorization process. The application does not request or store the user's Pinterest password. OAuth access tokens, when used, are treated as confidential credentials and should be stored securely and only used for authorized API operations.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the integration or maintain required operational records. The user may revoke the application's Pinterest authorization through Pinterest. Where locally stored integration data or credentials exist, they can be deleted from the application's storage or configuration.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials and data handled by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children's privacy

This personal integration is not directed to children under 13. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children for its own purposes.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning this application, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the GitHub repository where this policy is published.

11. Pinterest relationship

This application is an independent integration and is not operated, sponsored, or endorsed by Pinterest, Inc. Pinterest and its related trademarks belong to their respective owners.
